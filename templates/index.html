EPIOT: Electromobility Prediction Tool

Quantifying EV demand and flexibility for grid-aware decisions

What is this?

EPIOT helps you explore EV charging load and flexibility across custom areas. Draw regions on the map, pick a date, choose a strategy, and analyse profiles, KPIs and reports.

Main functions

  • Select up to 3 custom areas (draw on the map)
  • Predict EV load
  • Quantify up/down flexibility and compare scenarios
  • Explore KPI dashboards (user, grid, mobility, city, environment)
  • Export reports (.xlsx) for analysis & sharing
  • Inspect chargers & transformers on the map

Who is it for?

Charging Operators (CPOs) DSOs / City Energy Teams Urban Mobility Planners Researchers & Students Policymakers

How to use:

  1. Pan/zoom the map and draw up to three areas.
  2. Click Analyse
  3. Pick a date and charging strategy.
  4. Switch between Load Profiles, Flexibility, and KPIs, then download the report.
