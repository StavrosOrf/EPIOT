EPIOT

Electromobility Prediction Tool · quantifying EV demand and flexibility for grid-aware decisions

Docs GitHub DRIVE2X

Draw up to three areas on the map, pick a date, a session source and a charging strategy, and compare EV charging load, flexibility and KPIs side by side.

  • Custom areasUp to 3, drawn on the map
  • Load profilesCharging power over two days
  • FlexibilityUp and down headroom
  • KPI dashboardsUser, grid, mobility, city, environment
  • StrategiesFive rules, three session sources
  • ReportsExcel export and REST API

Results are synthetic scenarios with assumed demand, not verified forecasts of local charging.

Charging operators DSOs & city energy teams Mobility planners Researchers Policymakers

How to use

  1. Pan/zoom the map and draw up to three areas.
  2. Click Analyse.
  3. Pick a date, session source and charging strategy.
  4. Switch between Load profiles, Flexibility and KPIs, then download the report.
